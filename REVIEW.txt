CHECKED AND FOUND CONSISTENT (no action needed; for confidence)
- Mass 1.724 kg = sum of the plan components. CG 49.2 mm = 20.7 % MAC: MAC 194 mm and x_le_mac 9.0 mm for c/4 sweep 0 check out by hand.
- Inertia by hand: Ixx about 0.09, Iyy about 0.123, Izz about Ixx+Iyy; reported 0.086 / 0.127 / 0.210. Ixz = +int(xz dm) (FRD) is positive, as expected for a high aft V-tail and a low forward battery.
- Static margin: x_np 85.9 mm -> 18.9 % MAC, and -Cma/CLa gives 19.5 %. A hand NP estimate with a V-tail of Vh 0.39 gives 16-20 %. CLa 5.81/rad is about 8 % above the wing-alone Helmbold value (4.97) plus the tail, so it is plausible. AeroBuildup's Cma was correctly replaced by AVL's.
- Signs of Cmq, Clp, Cnr, Clb, Cnb, Clr, Cnp and CYb are standard. The per-surface control derivatives give the right force and moment directions: left aileron TE-down gives +Cl; left ruddervator TE-down gives +CY, -Cn, -Cm, +Cl. Cl_da 0.35/rad (hand 0.42), and pb/2V 0.255 checks out.
- Roll-mode tau 0.05 s is consistent with Lp = rho*V*S*b^2*Clp/(4*Ixx) = -19.8/s. Short period wn 9.3, zeta 0.71 and Dutch roll wn 3.5 are plausible.
- Static WOT: CT0 0.113 x 0.92 at 11,593 rpm gives 19.9 N (2.03 kg). Motor 34 A at 15.6 V and Kv 910 is self-consistent, and the SITL matches the propulsion stage. Cruise 30.3 W = 1.157 N x 13 m/s / 0.50 overall efficiency, excluding avionics.
- Spar: main tube 10x8 carries 68 % of M by EI share. Hand limit stress about 125 MPa vs 131 reported. Tip deflection of about 40 mm by hand vs 38 reported. Gust n 4.985 is reproduced by hand with a = 5.08.
- VTOL hover: momentum theory with FM 0.6 and eta 0.78 gives 390-480 W vs 415 W reported. The quad-X motor order and spin directions match ArduPilot.
- Frames: design-frame positions are fed to the JSBSim structural frame (x aft, z up, the same convention). The thrust direction is body FRD [1,0,0]. AERORP = CG, so Cm is about the CG. The sim model JSON takes mass, CG, inertia, derivatives and motor/prop/battery from the upstream results unchanged; the motor rm adds ESC r_on (0.08 + 0.004).
- derived.param: BATT_LOW_MAH 3200 = 10 Ah x (1-0.85+0.2x0.85). PTCH2SRV_RMAX 67 deg/s = (n-1)g/VNE. ROLL_LIMIT 45 (n 1.41).
- The gate engine never PASSes a None value (it becomes WARN), and NaN falls to the severity. GO is never printed with a FAIL, ERROR or BLOCKED.
