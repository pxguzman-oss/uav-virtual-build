clean tree (standard base, standard vtol, quick base). Each report now stamps the git hash and plan hash, and
records the fidelity and cache key of every stage.

HEADLINE RESULTS AFTER THE FIXES (standard fidelity, final code)

Base (fixed-wing, 1.72 kg): NO-GO. 6 FAIL, 25 WARN, 0 ERROR.
  Vs 9.25 m/s nominal, 9.75 m/s pessimistic.
  Range at 13 m/s (uncalibrated): conservative 139 km, band 139-176 km.
  Mission energy: 38-48 Wh, against 98 Wh usable.
  FAILs:
    - Pessimistic Vs is above hand-launch speed / 1.2 (11 m/s throw).
    - AIRSPEED_MIN / pessimistic Vs is 1.13 (needs >= 1.2).
    - In gusty wind, the launch dips below pessimistic Vs (min 8.8 m/s) and spends 1.1 s above the stall alpha.
    - LW-PLA Tg vs hot-day skin temperature.

VTOL (2.17 kg plan AUW): NO-GO. 4 FAIL, 22 WARN.
  Vs 10.4 m/s nominal, 11.0 m/s pessimistic.
  Range (uncalibrated): conservative 78 km, band 78-105 km.
  FAILs:
    - T/W 1.17 and hover duty 0.87 at the plan's 3.1 kg max-AUW case.
    - AIRSPEED_MIN / pessimistic Vs.
    - LW-PLA Tg.

RUNTIMES (2 cores): quick base 99 s; standard base 705 s warm (~30 min cold incl. ~17 min CFD); standard vtol 268 s.
